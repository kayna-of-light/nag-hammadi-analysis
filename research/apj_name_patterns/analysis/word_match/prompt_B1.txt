List 2 (parts)
A. right eye: sight; "the sight of the eye corresponds to the understanding"; those at the eyes are "intelligent and wise"
B. right breast: gives milk; at the breast are those who are in charity
C. teeth: bite and break food; correspond to natural truth: "they grind like a mill, and thus prepare the food"; the lowest natural things
D. left foot: stand and walk; those at the feet are the natural; "natural things are like the feet, on which the higher things rest"
E. left forearm: grasping, holding; strength of the arm; palms spread out signify supplication
F. kidneys: filter the blood; they "explore whether there is any useless and hurtful serum" and separate it; the reins signify truth, as the heart good
G. nails of the hands: hard outer covering of the fingertips; the outermost covering; those who give praise and blame without understanding
H. right ear: hearing; corresponds to simple obedience and perceptivity; the right ear to "perceptivity of truth from good"
I. right elbow: joint of the arm; the arms signify strength, the joint a joining
J. right shoulder: bears loads and moves the arm; "the shoulder corresponds to all power"
K. genitals: generation; correspond to conjugial love and the members allotted to generation
L. brain: seat of will and understanding, origin of the fibres; corresponds to beginnings and ends, the right hemisphere to the will of good, the left to the understanding
M. tonsils: guard the narrows between mouth and throat; a guard at an entrance that rejects or admits
N. left kidney: filters the blood; explores whether there is any useless and hurtful serum and separates it; the reins signify truth
