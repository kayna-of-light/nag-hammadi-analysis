I recognized several words as glosses of its angel names: Asterechme/star = right eye, Meniggesstroeth/meninges = brain, Ieronumos/holy name = right ear, Basiliademe/royal = tonsils, Amen/firm-true = teeth, Astrops/star = right breast, Kriman/judgment = nails of the hands, Biblo/book = kidneys, Trachoun/rough = left foot, Evanthen/well-flowering = left elbow, and probably Anesimalar/wind = spleen. So the sheet was not blind for me. My answers to Q3, Q11, Q20, Q23, Q25, Q28, Q31 and Q38 match those remembered assignments, and memory may have shaped them. Q16 does not match (I chose thigh; the list has tonsils). The parts listed for Q18 and Q39 do not include the ones I remember (spleen, elbow), so memory could not decide them.
