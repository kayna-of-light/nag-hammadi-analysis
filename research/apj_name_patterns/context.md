# Context: what the library already establishes about the names

**Sources**: literary-compilation `data/08_Correspondential_Texts/The Body That Waited for Breath_ The Angelic Anthropogony of the Apocryphon of John Read from the Coptic.md` (§§5–10, 12.4, 14); literary-compilation `data/08_Correspondential_Texts/The Unnamed Cartography_ A Systematic Validation of Swedenborgian Body-Part Correspondences Against the 365-Angel List of the Apocryphon of John.md` (§§3, 3.8); literary-compilation `docs/research_questions/apj_angel_list_coptic_reading.md`.

## The lists and their members

- The first list: 72 makers of the members, from the head to the toenails (II 15,29–17,6; IV 24,22–26,16). Codex II has 69 names, Codex IV supplies three and lacks two; the reconstruction is Quack's (*The Body That Waited for Breath* §5.2).
- The second list: 30 powers "active part by part in the members" (II 17,8–29), naming the part first and the power second (§7). About twenty-five of its members are also members of the first list, which is what makes Test 0 of the plan possible.
- The rulers: seven over the makers, seven over the movers, five over the faculties, four over the qualities with their mother, four demons with their mother, and Anaro (§§6, 8–10).

## What is known of the names

- 38 of 130 surviving names can be read at grade A or B (thesis §14.6). They are of two kinds: words that name the member's function (Amen for the teeth, κρίμα for the nails, *tbr* "break" for the shoulder, μήνιγγ- for the brain, ὄμμα for the imagination), and names of Egyptian decans and gods (Ptah the great, Horus the justified, Horus of Athribis, the decan Chnoumis, "the god").
- About 80 names match no decan form in Gundel's tables, no attestation in the ritual texts (Piwowarczyk 2021), and no Coptic word (Crum) (thesis §12.4).
- Piwowarczyk regards the lists as a later stratum of the long recension, added in Coptic transmission; Quack places their source in a Greek astrological treatise written in Egypt (thesis §§2.2, 11).

## What this means for the study

- The known decan and god names are a control group: their sounds come from Egyptian and are not expected to follow Swedenborgian meaning unless the compiler chose which name went to which member.
- The word-names already show a relation between name and function. Test 2 should not be counted as successful merely for rediscovering these: the plan's validation (Test 3) is on the second list.
