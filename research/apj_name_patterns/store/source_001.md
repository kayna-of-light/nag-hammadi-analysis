# Source 001 — Swedenborg's Grand Man chapters in *Arcana Coelestia*, and *Heaven and Hell* on heat and the quarters

**Citations**:
- Swedenborg, *Arcana Coelestia* (Potts translation), the continuations "concerning the Grand Man and correspondence" at the ends of the chapters of Genesis: §§3624–3649 (general), 3741–3751, 3883–3896 (heart and lungs), 4039–4055 (cerebrum and cerebellum), 4218–4228, 4318–4331 (the senses in general), 4403–4421 and 4523–4534 (eye and light), 4622–4634 (smell and nostrils), 4652–4660 (hearing and ears), 4791–4806 (taste, tongue and face), 4931–4953 (hands, arms, feet and loins), 5050–5062 (loins and members of generation), 5171–5190 and 5377–5396 (the interior viscera), 5552–5573 (skin, hair and bones), 5711–5727 (diseases); and §§3021, 3301, 3813, 3915, 9052, 10061.
- Swedenborg, *Heaven and Hell* (Ager translation) §§133–153 (heat; the four quarters).

**Accessed**: 2026-11-09. e-swedenborg static pages, `writings/static/d6943/<n>.htm` (*AC*) and `d5399/<n>.htm` (*HH*): 283 sections of *AC* and 21 of *HH*, read for the meaning table.

## Why it matters

These chapters are where Swedenborg sets out, member by member, which societies of heaven correspond to which parts of the body. They supply the meaning layer of the study: each member's spiritual counterpart is taken from here where he states it.

## Key statements (quoted)

**Correspondence is chiefly with function (*AC* §4223).** "although the heavens above mentioned do indeed correspond to the very organic forms of the human body … they nevertheless correspond chiefly to the functions of these viscera or organs … It is the functions therefore to which the heavenly societies chiefly correspond … This shows that the use existed before the organic forms of the body came forth; and that the use produced and adapted them to itself, and not the reverse." This is the basis for deriving a counterpart from function where Swedenborg does not treat a member.

**Placement of the societies (*AC* §4403).** "Those who appear above the head, and near it, are those who teach … Those who are under the back of the head are those who act silently and prudently. Those who are near the back act similarly, with a difference. Those who are at the chest or breast are those who are in charity. Those who are at the loins are those who are in conjugial love. Those who are at the feet are those who are natural, and those who are at the soles of the feet are the more gross of this kind … those who are at the nostrils are those who excel in perception, those who are at the ears are those who obey, and those who are at the eyes are those who are intelligent and wise."

**Head, body, feet (*AC* §4938).** "in the Grand Man celestial things constitute the head, spiritual the body, and natural the feet."

**Heart and lungs (*AC* §§3887–3889).** "They who are in the Lord's celestial kingdom all belong to the province of the heart … they who are in the spiritual kingdom all belong to the province of the lungs." "The will is that to which corresponds the pulsation of the heart … the understanding is that to which corresponds the respiration of the lungs." "the heart corresponds to the celestial which is of good, and the lungs to the spiritual which is of truth."

**Brain (*AC* §4052).** "they who are in the first principles or beginnings of good have relation to those things in the brain which are the beginnings, and are called the glands or cortical substances; whereas they who are in the first principles of truth relate to those things in the brains that flow out from these beginnings, and are called fibers … those who correspond to the right side of the brain are those who are in the will of good … those who correspond to the left side … are in the understanding of good and truth."

**The senses (*AC* §4404).** "the sense of touch corresponds to the affection of good, the sense of taste to the affection of knowing, the sense of smell to the affection of perceiving, the sense of hearing to the affection of learning, and also to obedience, and the sense of sight to the affection of understanding and of being wise."

**Tongue (*AC* §4791).** "The tongue affords entrance to the lungs, and also to the stomach … the tongue corresponds in general to the affection of truth … there are some who belong to the tongue itself, some to the larynx and the trachea, some to the throat, some to the gums, and some to the lips."

**Hands, arms, shoulders (*AC* §§4932–4934, 4937).** "They in the Grand Man who correspond to the hands and arms, and also to the shoulders, are those who have power by the truth of faith from good." "the arms signify strength, and the hands power." "the shoulder corresponds to all power."

**Feet (*AC* §4938).** "They in the Grand Man who correspond to the feet, the soles of the feet, and the heels, are they who are natural; wherefore by 'feet' in the Word are signified natural things … by 'soles of the feet' lower natural things, and by 'heels' the lowest natural things."

**Loins and generation (*AC* §§5050–5055, 5060).** "the loins and the members adhering to them correspond to genuine conjugial love." "There are heavenly societies to which correspond all and each of the members and organs allotted to generation in both sexes." "what and of what quality those heavenly societies are which belong to the several organs of generation, it has not been given me to know."

**Stomach (*AC* §§5174, 5176).** The food "is agitated in many ways, in order that its inner elements may be extracted … Such agitations are represented by the first agitations of spirits … in order that evils may be separated, and goods gathered to serve for use." "So long as spirits are in the state in which they are like food in the stomach, so long they are not in the Grand Man, but are being introduced into it."

**Liver (*AC* §5183).** "the liver draws in blood and separates it, pouring the better part into the veins … in embryos the liver receives the blood from the womb of the mother, and purifies it … thus acting as a guard before the heart."

**Spleen (*AC* §5184).** "They who are in the province of the Spleen are almost directly over the head; but their working falls on the organ in question." No function is described.

**Kidneys (*AC* §§5381, 5385).** "they explore the blood thrown into them to see whether there is any useless and hurtful serum in it, which they separate from what is useful." "the things which are of truth are signified by the 'reins,' and those which are of good by the 'heart.'"

**Intestines (*AC* §5392).** "the intestines are continued from the stomach, and the functions of the stomach become there more vigorous and harsh down to the last intestines."

**Skin (*AC* §§5553–5554).** "The societies to which the skins correspond are in the entrance to heaven … whom they either reject or admit; so that they may be called entrances or thresholds to heaven." The skin "reacts against internals."

**Bones and cartilages (*AC* §§5552, 5560, 5564–5565).** "the cartilages and bones which support and hold together all the parts of the body." "little life in the bones as compared with the soft parts which they enclose … as in the vertebrae and ribs in comparison with the heart and lungs." The cartilage "which serves as a support in front to the ribs." "bones still harder, as the teeth."

**Teeth (*AC* §§9052, 5387).** "'a tooth,' as being the exterior intellectual, and therefore natural truth … because they grind like a mill." "the beard and teeth signify the lowest natural things."

**Knees and thighs (*AC* §§3915, 3021, 5051).** "'knees,' or 'thighs,' as being the things that belong to conjugial love … thus the things that belong to the conjunction of the truth of faith with the good of love." "the 'thigh' signified conjugial love, and the 'hand' power."

**Flesh (*AC* §3813).** "the own of man's will made alive by the own of the Divine Human."

**Right and left (*AC* §10061).** "those things which are on the right side of man correspond to the good from which are truths, and those which are on the left correspond to the truths through which is good."

**Heat and the quarters (*HH* §§133, 136, 150).** "heat in its essence is love." "the heat of heaven constitutes the life of their will." "the east signifying love and its good clearly perceived, the west the same obscurely perceived, the south wisdom and intelligence in clear light, and the north the same in obscure light."

## What the chapters do not treat

No passage was found on the tonsils, the uvula, the neck as a province, the elbow, the fingers, the navel, the hypochondrium, the marrow as a province, the spleen's function, the arteries, the shins, the toes or the nails. For these the meaning table derives the counterpart from function and says so.
